About

I am a senior administrative and institutional management professional with over 25 years of experience in Government and premier autonomous institutions, including leadership responsibilities at institutions of national importance. I have served as Deputy Chief Executive (Administration) at the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata, contributing to the effective management of complex academic and administrative systems. My professional expertise encompasses institutional administration, legal affairs, financial management, vigilance, governance, regulatory compliance and strategic institutional development.

Throughout my career, I have undertaken significant responsibilities involving policy implementation, organisational leadership, administrative reforms, human resource management and the coordination of multidisciplinary institutional activities. I possess substantial experience in legal drafting, interpretation of statutory and regulatory provisions, arbitration, dispute resolution and the management of complex administrative and institutional matters. My approach combines legal understanding, administrative experience and strategic decision-making to promote transparency, accountability, efficiency and sound institutional governance.

With an interdisciplinary academic background in Science, Law and Education, I bring a broad perspective to institutional leadership, academic administration and organisational development. I remain committed to strengthening institutional systems, promoting ethical governance, facilitating academic excellence and contributing my professional experience to organisations pursuing public service, education, research and institutional advancement.
